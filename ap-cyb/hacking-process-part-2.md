A hacker is using a compromised teacher account to message the IT admin for admin access, pretending that they need a specific piece of data that they don't have access to. Which stage is this?

1. Taking Action 
2. Initial Access
3. Persistence
4. Lateral Movement

---

Which one is a lie?

1. Assets are anything of value to an organization, including data, people, and reputation.
2. Assets are all equally valuable, so each one should get the same level of protection.
3. Assets are often what an attacker is ultimately after when they take action on a target.

---

Reconnaissance in the hacking process is important because...

1. It gives the attacker a foothold on the network that they can return to later.
2. It shows the attacker which people, systems, and weaknesses are worth targeting.
3. It hides the attacker's activity so that defenders won't notice the attack.

---

Persistence in the hacking process is important because...

1. It lets the attacker spread from the compromised computer to others on the network.
2. It lets the attacker complete their goal, like stealing data or encrypting files.
3. It lets the attacker keep their access, even after a restart or a password change.

---

Why do we even do "Initial Access"?

1. To get a foothold inside the target, since most attacks can't proceed from the outside.
2. To learn which employees and systems in the organization are worth targeting.
3. To make sure the attacker can get back in, even if they're discovered and removed.
