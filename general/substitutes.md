# How Substitutes Are Picked

One class, one person at a time.

*TYAS leave system · revised 11/4/2026*

---

<!-- _class: flow -->

# The order

1. **Rules** Skip anyone who can't take it
2. **Pools** Go through 6 groups in order
3. **Ties** Least busy goes first
4. **Ask** One person at a time

---

# 1. Rules

Skipped unless **all** are true:

- At most **7 periods that day** and **28 that week**, counting this class
- Not on leave
- Free that period
- Not already covering that period, or asked for this class before

<br>

*Load = periods taught + coverage accepted or assigned.*

---

<!-- _class: pools -->

# 2. Pools

Next pool only when **nobody** is left in this one.

1. **Recommended:** the absent teacher's list, in their order
2. **Same division:** ES, MS, or HS
3. **Other divisions**
4. **Anyone:** any teacher or staff member
5. **Low Priority** list *(HR)*
6. **Non-Teaching Staff** list *(HR)*

---

# 3. Ties *(Pools 2–6)*

Each rule only breaks a tie in the one before:

1. Lightest load **that day**
2. Lightest load **that week**
3. Fewest periods covered **all time**
4. **Random**

---

# 4. Ask

- One person is asked at a time.
- **Declined** or **no answer 30 min before class** → next person.
- **Nobody left** → HR arranges cover.

<br>

Every offer says *why* that person was picked, and everyone can see it on the **Subbing Information** page.

---

# Exceptions

- **Co-taught classes:** no sub unless the teacher asks
- **Club time** (P9, Mon–Thu): HR arranges it
- **Learning Support** may cover their homeroom teacher's class over their own
- **HR and leadership** can override any pick
